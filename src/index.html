Greetings!! I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science at George Mason University. I work under Dr. Zoran Durić and Dr. Duminda Wijesekera at the Cyber Living Innovation Lab.

My research interest lies in algorithmic human behavioral analysis and computer vision. Currently I'm working on driver behavioral analysis in simulated and real-world driving. Another ongoing line of research is connected perception at signalized intersections using V2V and V2X sensor mapping.
